Before you take the wheel

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A few things to know before your next drive.

How do I enquire about a car?

Choose a car and open its details. Share your pickup area and travel dates, then send the prefilled WhatsApp message. Our team will confirm availability and rental terms.

Is a security deposit required?

Deposit requirements can vary by car and agreed rental terms. Check the car details and confirm the current policy, eligibility, and charges with the team.

What about fuel and included kilometres?

Included kilometres and extra-kilometre charges depend on the car. Fuel is not included unless your final quotation explicitly says otherwise.

Can you deliver the car to my area?

Contact the team with your exact address and pickup time. They will confirm coverage, availability, and any delivery charges.
